Gita Gopinath’s Per Jacobsson Foundation Lecture

Lecture delivered on the occasion of the Bank for International Settlements’ Annual General Meeting (June 2026)

← Back to Home

In her lecture, “Stablecoins and Anonymous Money,” Gita Gopinath examined how the rise of stablecoins is reshaping the landscape of money, payments, and financial regulation. Drawing on new research with Kyle Calder, Wenxin Du, and Jeremy Stein, she highlighted the widespread use of self-custody wallets and the limited ability of regulators to trace cross-border stablecoin transactions.

Gita Gopinath argued that the increasing use of more anonymous forms of digital money raises important challenges, from tax evasion and sanctions circumvention to capital controls and monetary sovereignty, and underscored the need for stronger international cooperation in stablecoin regulation.
