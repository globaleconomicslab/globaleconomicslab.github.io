G7

President Macron's G7 Economists Group on Global Imbalances

Professor Gita Gopinath was appointed by French President Macron, alongside economists Chong-En Bai (Tsinghua University), Hélène Rey (London Business School), and Axel Weber (Goethe University Frankfurt), to write a report on global imbalances as part of the French Presidency of the G7. The team of economists presented the report to the President of France on 30 March 2026, diagnosing the imbalances threatening global growth and recommending a cooperative, multilateral path to reduce them.

Our Impact Proposal

Three pathways to impact

There is no playbook for the new global economic order. We create cutting-edge research and turn it into insights that help policymakers and corporate leaders navigate change and make sound decisions.

1

Advising

Strategic advising to leaders in government, the private sector, think tanks, and international institutions. We bring cutting-edge research and deep policy experience to provide organizations with advice tailored to the realities of emerging technologies, economics, and politics.

2

Research

Directed, policy-relevant research on priority global economic issues, synthesized into digestible output and disseminated through op-eds, podcasts, books, and media appearances.

3

Teaching

Training and mentoring graduate and undergraduate students, and giving them real world experience.
